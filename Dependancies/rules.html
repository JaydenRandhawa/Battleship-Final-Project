Setting Up Your Board


  1. You get 4 ships, with lengths 5, 4, 3, and 2.
  2. To place a ship click a position on the board.
  3. Press "r" to set the orientation of a ship before you place it.
  4. Check the footer instruction panel to see the next ship size and current orientation.
  5. Once you have placed all of your ships, you can create a preset
    in the menu bar "Game > Make Preset From Layout"




Playing the Game


  1. Click anywhere on the opponent board to guess.
  2. If your click was a hit, you can make another guess.
  3. If your click was a miss, the computer will guess, following the same rules




Game End Contitions


  1. The game ends if wither player gets 14 hits.
  2. The game may end early if you press restart in the game menu(Game > Restart)
    or close the game window.
